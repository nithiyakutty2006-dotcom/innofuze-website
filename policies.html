Registration and enquiry forms ask for a name, email address, and mobile number; the chat form also asks for a message. Do not submit sensitive personal, payment, or account credentials through these forms.

To ask about a submitted request or personal information, email innofuzetech249@gmail.com.

Use the site and its forms for genuine service enquiries and lawful purposes. Do not attempt to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access to the website or its systems.
